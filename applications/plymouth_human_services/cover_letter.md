Dear Commissioner Bratti:

I am writing to apply for the Human Services leadership role supporting your oversight across Public Health, Veterans' Services, Elder Affairs, the Disabilities Commission, the Public Library, the Youth Commission, the civic inclusion committee, and Recreation. I bring graduate preparation in applied economics and public health, public-sector and emergency-response experience, and a data-systems background suited to a department that must plan services, evaluate community needs, coordinate across divisions, and compete for grant funding.

My strongest fit is the combination of health systems thinking, operational accountability, and evidence-based decision support. Through graduate work in public health and applied economics, I have developed the ability to translate population-level problems into measurable service priorities, analyze trends across local and broader datasets, and prepare clear written products for decision-makers. My work has emphasized water access, infrastructure risk, health-system strain, longitudinal social and economic outcomes, and reproducible data workflows using R, Python, LaTeX, and Git-based documentation. That background aligns closely with the Town's need for statistical tools, internal and external database support, trend analysis, and data-driven recommendations.

I also bring practical public-service experience in environments where policy, documentation, judgment, and cross-functional coordination matter. As a Regional Disaster Dispatcher with the American Red Cross, I supported incident triage, team activation, escalation workflows, and communications needed to align resources with urgent community needs. As a Correction Officer with the Massachusetts Department of Correction, I worked in a high-accountability human-services and public-safety setting involving respectful population management, crisis response, confidentiality, documentation, and coordination across security, health-adjacent, and administrative functions. My prior Air Force experience further strengthened disciplined information handling, mission support, and work under clearly defined legal and policy constraints.

The Plymouth role is especially compelling because it sits at the intersection of public health, aging, disability access, veterans' services, youth development, recreation, civic inclusion, and municipal operations. I would be prepared to support your leadership by strengthening service planning, improving data tracking, organizing grant and compliance workflows, monitoring state and federal policy developments, and helping translate evidence into practical programs. I would also approach the ADA Coordinator function with the seriousness it deserves: as a core municipal access, equity, communication, and implementation responsibility that should be integrated across departments rather than treated as a narrow compliance task.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in public health systems, analytics, emergency response, and accountable public-sector operations can support the Town of Plymouth's Human Services Department. Thank you for your consideration.
