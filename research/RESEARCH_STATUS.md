## Portfolio thesis

This research portfolio uses reproducible evidence workflows to connect public health, infrastructure, family economics, environmental risk, technical diagnostics, and archive documentation. The common operating model is controlled movement from raw records and secondary sources into validated claims, decision-support tools, manuscripts, and public-facing research artifacts.
