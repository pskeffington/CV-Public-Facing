# Research Status

Public-facing overview of current research, technical methods, manuscripts, and research-engineering development.

## Portfolio thesis

This portfolio is organized around translational health data science, longitudinal clinical data, public-health informatics, biomedical signal and imaging methods, reproducible research engineering, rural and environmental health, GIS and spatial analysis, longitudinal population-health research, and evidence-governed analytical workflows.

## Current research

### CART-TRACE — Final public scholarly freeze

Synthetic-first translational-health data-science capstone reconstructing post-infusion hospital care trajectories following CAR T-cell therapy using treatment-relative episodes, deterministic care-state reconstruction, provenance-preserving mapping, explicit missingness behavior, and reproducible utilization metrics. The public capstone package is frozen and internally validated against controlled synthetic truth. Institutional patient-level validation and external clinical validity are not established.

A separate synthetic administrative access-gating extension now studies how referral, program-review, facility, network, payer, Medicare, financial-clearance, and derived access states can be represented as auditable longitudinal administrative events. This extension is developing methods research only; it does not determine clinical eligibility, payer authorization, financial approval, or treatment readiness.

### Family economics, financial literacy, and fertility — Pre-submission review

Longitudinal NLSY79 manuscript work using reproducible fixed-effects, count, and survival-model workflows with model-specific sample audits and source-to-claim controls. Current work is focused on manuscript cleanup, citation verification, model-table parity, and blinded-package review before submission.

### Rural water, wastewater, and infrastructure health — Applied practicum completed

Completed applied rural public-health work integrating GIS terrain/access analysis, water and wastewater evidence, stakeholder documentation, source validation, and implementation-oriented outputs. Public references should describe the completed practicum contribution without implying that the underlying private repository is a public source surface.

### Humanitarian WASH and health-system disruption — Methods-note candidate

Global-health work connecting WASH, displacement, environmental-health, geospatial evidence, and health-system disruption through a structured, source-bounded secondary-data framework.

### WASH systems, watershed stress, and spatial equity — Active research

Environmental-health and spatial-equity research using public-source WASH, watershed, service-reliability, governance, GIS, and spatial data. Outputs remain research observations rather than operational infrastructure direction.

### Machine learning and evidence evaluation — Active methods development

Machine-learning-assisted evidence organization, feature and benchmark design, deterministic validation, source-to-claim controls, human-review gates, and auditable analytical outputs.

### Biomedical data science methods — Developing

Supporting work includes an executable ECG Phase 1 benchmark pathway with comparative freeze still pending, PET imaging/radiomics robustness research in pre-analysis status, and an open-data cancer end-of-life manuscript scaffold in source-validation mode.
