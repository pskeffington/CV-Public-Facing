# Research Status

Public-facing overview of current research, technical methods, manuscripts, and research-engineering development.

## Portfolio thesis

This portfolio is organized around translational health data science, longitudinal clinical data, public-health informatics, biomedical signal and imaging methods, reproducible research engineering, rural and environmental health, GIS and spatial analysis, longitudinal population-health research, and evidence-governed analytical workflows.

## Current research

### CART-TRACE — Final public scholarly freeze

Synthetic-first translational-health data-science capstone reconstructing post-infusion hospital care trajectories following CAR T-cell therapy using treatment-relative episodes, deterministic care-state reconstruction, provenance-preserving mapping, explicit missingness behavior, and reproducible utilization metrics. The public scholarly package is frozen; any institutional clinical-data extension remains separately governed and contingent on authorization and data access.

### Family economics, financial literacy, and fertility — Under peer review

Longitudinal NLSY79 manuscript work using reproducible fixed-effects, count, and survival-model workflows with model-specific sample audits and source-to-claim controls.

### Rural water, wastewater, and infrastructure health — Customer-facing project completed and accepted / final oral-media delivery pending

Completed applied rural public-health work integrating GIS terrain/access analysis, water and wastewater evidence, stakeholder documentation, source validation, and decision-support outputs. Manuscript refinement, added GIS validation, and further source verification are scholarly follow-on work rather than practicum-completion blockers.

### Humanitarian WASH and health-system disruption — Presentation complete / methods-note candidate

Global-health work connecting WASH, displacement, environmental-health, geospatial evidence, and health-system disruption through a structured secondary-data framework.

### Machine learning and evidence evaluation — Active methods development

Machine-learning-assisted evidence organization, feature and benchmark design, deterministic validation, source-to-claim controls, human-review gates, and auditable analytical outputs.

### Biomedical data science methods — Developing

Supporting work on ECG signal quality, PET imaging robustness, cancer outcomes, and reproducible biomedical study design using public-data pathways and explicit validation boundaries.
