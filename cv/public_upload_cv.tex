\section*{Research Interests and Current Projects}
\subsection*{Life-Course Data, Family Economics, and Fertility}
Examines how financial preparedness, reproductive education, marital stability, fertility, and timing of first birth interact across the life course. Current work uses fixed-effects modeling, count models, survival analysis, model-specific sample audits, and reproducible manuscript workflows.

\subsection*{Rural Water, Wastewater, and Infrastructure-Linked Health Risk}
Develops planning materials for rural public-health infrastructure problems where terrain, access, aging systems, and fragmented records complicate prioritization. Current work combines source inventories, official-source validation tracking, GIS processing, terrain/access scoring, and stream-aware exposure/access prototype indices.

\subsection*{Humanitarian WASH and Health-System Disruption}
Organizes secondary-data frameworks for public-health planning in disrupted environments, arranging WASH, health-facility, displacement, environmental-health, and geospatial source classes into transparent decision-support domains.

\subsection*{Reproducible Computational Diagnostics}
Explores computational diagnostics at the intersection of applied cryptography, topology, and reproducible methods, including persistent-homology features as auxiliary diagnostics for structured or biased controls.
