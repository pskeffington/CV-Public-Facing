\section*{Research Program}
\subsection*{Life-Course Data, Family Economics, and Fertility}
Uses longitudinal public-use cohort data to study how financial preparedness, reproductive education, marital stability, fertility, and timing of first birth interact across the life course. Current work with the NLSY79 combines fixed-effects modeling, count models, survival analysis, model-specific sample audits, and reproducible LaTeX/R workflows.

\subsection*{Rural Water, Wastewater, and Infrastructure-Linked Health Risk}
Builds decision-support tools for rural public-health infrastructure problems where terrain, access, aging systems, and fragmented records complicate prioritization. Current McDowell County, West Virginia work integrates source inventories, official-source validation tracking, GIS processing, terrain/access scoring, and stream-aware wastewater exposure/access prototype indices.

\subsection*{Humanitarian WASH and Health-System Disruption}
Develops rapid secondary-data frameworks for public-health planning in disrupted environments. Gaza WASH work organizes public humanitarian, WASH, health-facility, displacement, environmental-health, and geospatial source classes into transparent decision-support domains.

\subsection*{Reproducible Computational Diagnostics}
Develops computational diagnostics at the intersection of applied cryptography, topology, and reproducible methods. Current work evaluates persistent-homology features as auxiliary diagnostics for structured, weak, or intentionally biased controls.
