\section*{Research Statement}
My work focuses on building reproducible data and evidence systems for public-health, health-services, environmental, and scholarly research. I combine GIS, longitudinal and econometric methods, machine-learning-assisted evidence workflows, Python/R automation, structured provenance, and human review to make complex research inputs more traceable and usable for population-health and scholarly decision support.

Current projects span rural water and wastewater infrastructure, longitudinal family and fertility research, humanitarian WASH, biomedical methods, and research-engineering systems. A parallel digital-humanities program, TransHeb, develops structured corpus and witness models, transcription and normalization workflows, linguistic-processing scaffolds, provenance controls, reviewer workflows, and publication exports for Hebrew manuscript research.
